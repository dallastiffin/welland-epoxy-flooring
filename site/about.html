Why We Stay In South Niagara

Working one area makes you better at it. We know what the slabs here do because we open them up every week.

We know which streets have pre war concrete under them and which subdivisions were poured last year. We know that a basement off Ontario Road behaves differently to one in a new build off Rice Road, and why.

An installer covering half the province cannot know that, and it shows in what they miss.

It also means we are twenty minutes away if something needs looking at afterwards.

What We Learned From This City's Ground

Welland taught us to test everything.

The clay plain under this city holds water against the underside of slabs, and the water table sits high enough that a wet spring puts groundwater within inches of a lot of footings. Concrete wicks that water and passes it upward as vapour.

Coat a slab like that without checking and the floor comes off, sometimes within a year. It looks like a product failure. It is not. It is an untested slab.

So the humidity probe goes into every floor we quote, and the number goes on the paperwork. It costs us a few minutes and it has saved a lot of people a second installation.

We would rather tell you your basement is not ready than take the job and hope.

How We Price And What You Get In Writing

We measure the floor. We do not estimate it off a photograph and we do not price by the room.

You get a written specification that names the products, the number of coats, the preparation method and the moisture reading we took. That means you can compare us properly against another quote instead of comparing two numbers with nothing behind them.

If a competitor is cheaper, that document usually shows you why.

Want to know what your floor needs? We will come and measure it.

What We Will Not Do

We will not acid etch. It leaves residue, it gives an inconsistent profile, and it is behind most of the peeling floors we get called to strip.

We will not coat over a failing layer and call it a repair.

We will not lay an exterior floor in unsuitable weather to hit a date.

We will not take on a basement where water is actively coming in until that is dealt with. We will explain what you are looking at and who to talk to.

Turning work away is not good for a week's income. It is very good for the next five years of referrals.

What We Install

More Floors We Build

Other systems we install across Welland and south Niagara.

Garage Floor Coating

A coated garage floor stops the slab absorbing the brine your car carries in, and that is most of the battle in Welland. Bare concrete drinks salt water all winter, and the freeze and thaw that follows breaks the surface apart from the inside.

View Garage Floor Coating

Basement Floor Coating

A basement floor in Welland sits on wet clay with a high water table under it, which makes it the most demanding slab we coat in this city. Done properly it turns a cold grey box into a room you will actually use. Done without testing, it is the floor most likely to fail.

View Basement Floor Coating

Flake Epoxy Flooring

Flake epoxy is a resin base with coloured vinyl chip scattered into it while it is wet, sealed under a clear top coat. It is the finish most people picture when they think of a coated garage, and it is what we install most often across Welland.

View Flake Epoxy Flooring

Polyaspartic Floor Coating

Polyaspartic hardens quickly, tolerates slab temperatures that would leave epoxy soft, and keeps its colour in daylight. On a Welland garage, where the slab is cold half the year and the door is open to the sun, it is usually the right top coat.

View Polyaspartic Floor Coating

Concrete Floor Coating

Coating concrete is mostly a preparation job. The resin is the easy part. What decides whether the floor lasts is what happens to the slab in the hours before anything is poured onto it.

View Concrete Floor Coating

Epoxy Floor Repair and Recoating

If your coating is peeling, bubbling or lifting at the edges, the resin is usually not what failed. Something underneath it did, and putting a fresh coat over the top will buy you a year at best.

View Epoxy Floor Repair and Recoating

Commercial Epoxy Flooring

A commercial floor has to take trolleys, foot traffic, spills and cleaning chemicals, and it has to keep doing it without closing you down for a week. We build resin floors for shops, units, workshops and back of house areas across Welland and south Niagara.

View Commercial Epoxy Flooring

Industrial and Warehouse Flooring

Welland has been a manufacturing town since the canal was cut, and it is building again. There are six business parks in the city with a seventh in development, and the advanced manufacturing base has been growing rather than shrinking.

View Industrial and Warehouse Flooring

Patio and Outdoor Concrete Coating

A coated patio, porch or walkway seals the concrete against the water that would otherwise get into it and freeze. Outdoors is where Welland's freeze and thaw does its most visible damage, and an uncoated slab shows it within a few winters.

View Patio and Outdoor Concrete Coating

Metallic Epoxy Flooring

Metallic epoxy uses reflective pigment suspended in clear resin, worked while it is wet so the colour moves and pools. The result reads like polished stone or poured marble, and no two floors come out the same.
