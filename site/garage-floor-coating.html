Garage Floor Coating

Garage Floor Coating In Welland, Ontario

A coated garage floor stops the slab absorbing the brine your car carries in, and that is most of the battle in Welland. Bare concrete drinks salt water all winter, and the freeze and thaw that follows breaks the surface apart from the inside.

The damage is predictable. It starts as a pitted strip just inside the door where the car sits and drips, then works back along the tire tracks. Once it is pitting it does not stop on its own.

Garage Floor Coating details

Why Welland Garages Pit Faster Than They Should

The cycle matters more than the cold. January here averages a high near minus one and a half, so the slab spends the season crossing back and forth over freezing rather than staying frozen.

Water in the pores of the concrete expands each time it turns to ice. Do that a hundred times over a winter and the paste holding the aggregate together gives up.

Add road salt and it gets worse, because brine keeps melting at temperatures where plain water would stay solid. That means more cycles, not fewer.

An attached garage has one more problem. The slab is often the coldest concrete in the house and it sits on the same wet clay as the basement, so it gets moisture from below as well as salt from above.

What We Put Down

We grind the slab with diamond tooling to open the surface. Acid etching does not give a reliable profile and it is a common reason coatings peel, so we do not use it.

Cracks are routed, filled with a rigid resin and ground back level.

We take a humidity reading inside the slab and choose the primer to match it. On older Welland garages that reading decides the whole build.

Then the base coat, with vinyl flake broadcast into it until the floor refuses any more. A day later the surplus is scraped off and vacuumed up, and a polyaspartic top coat goes over it.

The flake is not only cosmetic. It gives grip on a floor that gets wet in winter, and it hides the ordinary marks a working garage collects.

Colours And Grip

Most people take a grey or a charcoal blend. Blues, oranges and tans are all available, and a fuller chip broadcast hides more.

If the garage doubles as a workshop or a gym we can add grit to the top coat for extra traction. It is worth asking for on a floor that gets wet often.

Timing And Access

A standard double garage is two days on site. You keep off it overnight between the coats.

Walk on it the evening of the second day. Park on it after about three days, and we will confirm the exact times for your build before we leave.

Basement Floor Coating

A basement floor in Welland sits on wet clay with a high water table under it, which makes it the most demanding slab we coat in this city. Done properly it turns a cold grey box into a room you will actually use. Done without testing, it is the floor most likely to fail.

Flake Epoxy Flooring

Flake epoxy is a resin base with coloured vinyl chip scattered into it while it is wet, sealed under a clear top coat. It is the finish most people picture when they think of a coated garage, and it is what we install most often across Welland.

Polyaspartic Floor Coating

Polyaspartic hardens quickly, tolerates slab temperatures that would leave epoxy soft, and keeps its colour in daylight. On a Welland garage, where the slab is cold half the year and the door is open to the sun, it is usually the right top coat.
