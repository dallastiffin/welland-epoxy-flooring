Contact details and what to expect

What Happens When We Come Out

We measure the floor rather than pace it.

We drill a small hole and take a relative humidity reading inside the slab. It takes a few minutes, it costs nothing, and on Welland concrete it is the reading that decides the build.

We look at the cracks, the joints, the drainage and anything already coated onto the slab.

The specification and the price come back to you in writing, usually the same day.

A Photo Saves Everyone Time

Send a picture before we visit and we can often tell you straight away what you are dealing with. A wide shot of the whole floor plus a close up of the worst area is the useful pair.

It counts for most on a floor that has already gone wrong, since how the damage is distributed points to the cause.
